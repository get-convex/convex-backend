<Admonition type="caution" title="Directory Sync is in beta">

Directory Sync is a [beta feature](/production/state/#beta-features) available
on the Convex Business and Enterprise plans. To have it turned on for your team,
contact Convex support.

</Admonition>

Directory Sync connects your identity provider's user directory to your Convex
team. Your directory becomes the source of truth for who belongs to the team and
what team role they hold.

Directory Sync is configured on the
[Team Authentication page](https://dashboard.convex.dev/team/settings/team-authentication),
under **Team Settings → Team Authentication**.

<Admonition type="info">

Directory Sync and [SSO](/team-management/sso.mdx) are separate features and can
be used independently, though most teams use both. They share the
**Authentication Domains** section of the page.

</Admonition>

## Setting up Directory Sync

### 1. Verify a domain

Directory Sync only manages members whose Convex account uses an email on a
domain your team has verified. If you haven't verified one yet, do that first.
See [verifying a domain](/team-management/sso.mdx#1-verify-a-domain).

### 2. Connect your directory

Select **Configure** in the **Directory sync** section.

<Screenshot
  story="docs/pages/TeamAuthentication#Directory Sync"
  alt="The Directory Sync section before a directory has been configured"
/>

Convex first explains what connecting a directory does, then hands you off to a
separate configuration page in a new tab, where you pick your identity provider
and follow its instructions to point the directory at Convex.

<Screenshot
  story="docs/pages/TeamAuthentication#Directory Sync Configure"
  alt="The dialog that explains the identity provider step before configuring a directory"
/>

Convex reports the connection's progress while you work through that page, and
tells you once the directory is linked. You can close the dialog and come back
later without losing anything.

### 3. Wait for the initial sync

Your identity provider sends the directory's groups and users over after the
connection is made. This can take up to an hour, but is normally ready within a
few minutes. Groups appear in **Directory group roles** as they arrive.

<Screenshot
  story="docs/pages/TeamAuthentication#Directory Sync Initial Sync"
  alt="The Directory Sync section during the initial sync, with no groups yet"
/>

### 4. Map directory groups to roles

Each group in your directory maps to a Convex team role. Use the edit button
next to a group to change the role it gives its members.

<Screenshot
  story="docs/pages/TeamAuthentication#Directory Group Roles"
  alt="Directory group roles, mapping each directory group to a Convex role"
/>

A group can map to:

- **Admin** — full access to the team.
- **Developer** — the default. Groups you never map give Developer.
- One or more [custom roles](/team-management/custom-roles.mdx).

<Screenshot
  story="docs/pages/TeamAuthentication#Edit Group Role"
  alt="The dialog for choosing the Convex role a directory group gives"
/>

A user often belongs to several groups. Convex resolves their role like this:

1. **Admin**, if any of their groups maps to Admin.
2. Otherwise, the union of the custom roles granted by all of their groups, if
   any group grants one.
3. Otherwise, **Developer**.

A group named `convex-team-admins` is reserved: it always gives Admin, and its
mapping can't be edited. See
[Emergency lockout recovery](#emergency-lockout-recovery).

### 5. Review the changes and enable

Directory Sync does not manage anyone until you turn it on. Select **Review** on
the Directory Sync section to see exactly what enabling it would do.

<Screenshot
  story="docs/pages/TeamAuthentication#Directory Sync Synced"
  alt="The Directory Sync section asking you to review role mappings before enabling"
/>

This dialog lists every current team member and every user in the directory,
with the role each one holds today and the role the directory would give them:

<Screenshot
  story="docs/pages/TeamAuthentication#Review Directory Changes"
  alt="The review dialog listing each member's current role and the role the directory would give them"
/>

- Members the directory contains take the role their groups give them, which may
  be different than the role they hold today.
- Members the directory doesn't list are marked **Not in directory** and are
  left alone. They keep their access and their role until you remove them from
  the team or add them to the directory.
- Directory users who aren't on the team yet are marked **Not in team**. Once
  Directory Sync is enabled, they may choose to join the team by selecting the
  team invitation from the team switcher in the Convex dashboard.
- Users your directory doesn't hold active are marked **Suspended in
  directory**. A member linked to one is removed from the team; a non-member is
  not offered it.

Check the acknowledgement and select **Enable directory sync**. The prompt to
review goes away, and the directory starts managing the members it covers.

<Screenshot
  story="docs/pages/TeamAuthentication#Directory Sync Enabled"
  alt="The Directory Sync section with directory management enabled"
/>

## How members join

Directory Sync offers the team rather than adding people to it. A directory user
is offered your team once they have a Convex account with a **verified email**
matching their directory entry, on one of your verified domains.

The team shows up as an invitation in their team switcher, which they select to
accept it:

<Screenshot
  story="docs/pages/Projects#Team Switcher With Invitation"
  alt="A directory-synced team offered as an invitation in the team switcher"
/>

It is also always listed under **Available Teams** on their
[profile page](https://dashboard.convex.dev/profile), including after they
dismiss it in the switcher:

<Screenshot
  story="docs/pages/Profile#Available Teams"

To see who has been offered the team but hasn't joined, use **⋮ → View pending

## Keeping the team in sync

Once Directory Sync is enabled, changes in your identity provider flow into
Convex on their own:

- Adding a user to the directory offers them the team.
- Moving a user between groups changes their Convex role.
- Changing a group's mapping re-applies to everyone that group covers.
- Suspending a user in the directory, or removing them from it, takes them off
  the Convex team. See [Things to know](#things-to-know) for what that costs
  them.

Changes can take up to an hour to arrive, so your Convex team can lag behind
your identity provider.

Your directory also takes over the parts of the members page it covers:

- Once Directory Sync is enabled, the team stops accepting manual invitations.
  Add the person to your directory instead. Invitations sent before the
  directory was connected can no longer be accepted either.
- Once Directory Sync is enabled, you can't change the role of a member the
  directory manages, or remove them from the team, from the Convex dashboard.
  Move them between groups, or take them out of the directory, in your identity
  provider instead.
- Members the directory doesn't cover are unaffected, and anyone can still
  remove themselves from the team.

To manage the directory connection, view the current status of the directory, or
request a manual sync, use the **⋮ → Manage** button.

## Disabling Directory Sync

**⋮ → Disable Directory Sync** disconnects the directory. Members your identity
provider provisioned keep their team membership and the role they hold at that
moment.

The team is freed from directory management right away, but the directory itself
can take up to an hour to disappear from this page while Convex finishes tearing
the connection down.

Deleting a verified domain has a similar effect for the members on it: the
directory can no longer manage them.

## Emergency lockout recovery

Mapping every directory group to a non-admin role or removing all users from the
directory leaves the team with no admin, and nobody left who can map one back.
The same happens if the groups your admins belong to stop giving Admin. A
reserved group name exists so you can always get back in:

1. In your identity provider, create a directory group named
   `convex-team-admins` (the match is case-insensitive).
2. Add the people who should be team admins to it.
3. Wait for the group to sync. It appears under **Directory group roles** with
   the role **Admin**, and the edit button next to it is disabled: this mapping
   is fixed and can't be changed from Convex.

## Things to know

**Project roles are managed separately.** Your directory sets a member's team
role. [Project Admin](/dashboard/teams/teams.mdx#project-admins) is granted per
project on the members page.

**Deprovisioning is destructive.** Suspending a user in your directory, or
removing them from it, takes them off the Convex team. They lose access
immediately, and their membership goes with it. Making them active in the
directory again offers them the team, but their project roles have to be set up
from scratch.

**Deleting a group in your directory deletes its role mapping**, including any
custom roles mapped to it. If you add the group back later it arrives unmapped,
which gives Developer, so its role and custom roles have to be set again. The
same is true for a group you rename, which your identity provider may send as a
delete and an add.

**A custom role can't be deleted while a directory group maps to it.** Remap
those groups first, then delete the role.

**Members are matched to directory users by verified email.** A directory user
only links to a Convex account that has verified the email your directory
carries for them. If that email stops being verified on the account, that member
will be removed from your Convex team.

## Who can configure Directory Sync

Team Admins can do everything on this page. Team Developers can see the
configuration but not change it. With
[custom roles](/team-management/custom-roles.mdx) you can grant the individual
[role actions](/team-management/role-actions.mdx#directory-sync):
`directorySync:view`, `directorySync:enable`, `directorySync:disable`,
`directorySync:updateGroupMapping`, and `directorySync:deleteGroupMapping`.
Reviewing the roster also needs `member:view`, since it names each team member
and the role they hold.

Enabling and disabling Directory Sync, and every change to a group's role
mapping, is recorded in the
[team audit log](/dashboard/teams/teams.mdx#audit-log).
